Pepperoni Pizza

Description

Originally from Naples, Italy, the pizza is ubiquitous world-wide. While its base is made from dough, cheese, and tomato sauce, it can be topped with virtually anything!

Ingredients

  • 16 oz pizza dough
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce (see below)
  • 20 slices pepperoni
  • 12 oz mozzarella cheese, grated
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon fresh oregano (optional)
  • flour for rolling and shaping dough

Pizza Sauce:

  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon granluated garlic
  • 1/2 teaspoon granulated onion
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 500 degrees F.
  2. Mix in a bowl the pizza sauce ingredients.
  3. Roll out dough on a lightly floured surface.
  4. Transfer the dough to a large cast-iron skillet.
  5. Add sauce in a light layer over the pizza, leaving about a 1/4-inch crust around the edges.
  6. Sprinkle half the pepperoni over the sauce.
  7. Top the pizza with grated cheese and the rest of the pepperoni, then season with black pepper.
  8. Place the skillet over a high heat burner for 2 minutes to get it preheated and the crust cooking right away.
  9. Transfer to the oven and bake for 10-12 minutes.
  10. Let the pizza rest for 1-2 minutes on a cutting board.
  11. Cut into slices, top with oregano, and serve hot!
